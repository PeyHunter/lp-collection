# Discogs is the only way to log in

Users log in with their Discogs account (OAuth) and LP-Collection keeps no passwords of its own. Every Copy is mirrored into the Owner's Discogs collection, and Discogs only gives suggested prices to a logged-in user with seller settings filled in, so every User needs a Discogs connection anyway. One login avoids storing passwords and keeps each User's Discogs requests under their own rate limit. The cost is that someone without a Discogs account can't use the app until they make one (it's free).
