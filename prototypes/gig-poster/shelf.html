How the shelf works

  1. Photograph your shelf straight on, the whole thing in one picture.
  2. Line up the grid with the squares. Columns get letters, rows get numbers.
  3. Put Copies in squares when you add them, or a whole square at once.
  4. Look up any record and its square lights up on the photo.
